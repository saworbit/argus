The upper deathmatch spawn at (880 128 24) was missing a usable graph exit.
The candidate seats a route through the middle ledge to the lower deck.
The tests also exposed a gap in routing from the middle shelf at z -104
and a dry eastward drop that could become a lava entry when the bot turned
south in the air. [Spawn issue](https://github.com/saworbit/argus/issues/789)

Each row below uses three matched 185-second pairs. Lava is the total
BSP-backed count, and route failures are medians. Each comparison has its

## Full regeneration

The e1m8 seat sidecar keeps the tested splice at the top level and records
the full generation seats and walks under `regeneration`. The fresh
sampler omits at least one endpoint of each of the eight splice cuts, so
those cuts apply only to the splice. The two earlier puppet convictions
also remain in the probe sidecar. Each generation still checks its own
walk and cut endpoints before publishing either graph file.

To reproduce, point `ARGUS_SRC` at a private copy of `src` and run
`argus-mcp nav e1m8 --no-register` twice with the stock BSP. Both runs
produced the same 196-waypoint QC and JSON pair. The budget is 452 of 600
edicts including the reserve. All six settled deathmatch starts are within
92u of a seat and the single-player start is within 138u.

## West-floor route to the rocket shelf

The private stock-BSP fixture routed from both west-floor starts to the
rocket shelf at `(1249 -127 -552)`. It added ten ordinary walks around the
basin and a jump from `(1249 -439 -712)`. The normal router chose the
approach. A private handler checked the line-up for collision, then checked
the landing with a six-second horizon before launching north at 70 u/s.

| Start | Played cadence | 100 ms cadence |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `(673 321 -712)` | 11.9 to 15.5 s | 11.4 s |
| `(673 544 -712)` | 18.8 to 28.2 s | 33.8 s |

Each cell has two repeats on fixture build `a4e06289cce0`. All eight
finished within 35 seconds and landed on dry world support at full health.
The trace records ordinary arrival, fixed-task completion and landing
as three separate events. Ordinary arrival cleared the steering goal during
descent, and the fixed task completed before contact. Four runs on the same
graph with the new hop and stock jump handling timed out on build
`c328410b4811`.

The 152-seat splice preserves the existing ungated reach from all seven
settled spawns. The refused west-face links stayed in place. Adoption still
requires ordinary routing and a registered played ladder that covers the
middle shelf and the separate combat approach.

## Northern-shelf landing prediction

In a private stock-BSP test, the bot kept its previous air velocity when
the requested change moved the projected landing from dry support to
liquid. At played cadence the candidate reduced north-shelf lava entries
from two to one in four approaches. At 100 ms both arms entered lava in
two of four approaches. The four east-deck controls stayed dry in each arm
at both cadences. The candidate stayed local after the remaining north-shelf
misses. [Landing trial](https://github.com/saworbit/argus/issues/952#issuecomment-7051582365)

The upper deathmatch spawn at (880 128 24) was missing a usable graph exit.
The candidate seats a route through the middle ledge to the lower deck.
The tests also exposed a gap in routing from the middle shelf at z -104
and a dry eastward drop that could become a lava entry when the bot turned
south in the air. [Spawn issue](https://github.com/saworbit/argus/issues/789)

BSP-backed count, and route failures are medians. Each comparison has its
own control.

seven settled deathmatch spawns. Its lowest directed
reach is 119 of 142 seats, or 107 without equipment and skill gates.

upper-spawn starts. These results describe the tested splice onto the
shipped graph. Full regeneration refused the shelf connector because
(833 -159 -368) was missing from the newly generated graph. Main's existing
sidecar also refused regeneration on a missing cut endpoint.

a northern-shelf fall and a fall after midair combat damage. Cutting three
engine-refused shortcuts from n84 left both west-to-rocket-launcher tests

approaches to zero, but that candidate stayed local after the played route

# E1m8 middle shelf and east drop

The original #947 candidate recorded 36 of its 45 route failures on the
unseated shelf at z -104. The shelf splice adds seven seats and a route to
the lower deck. Its short floor probe can end in open space before the
lower floor starts. The existing e1m8 deep-drop guard may look 72u ahead
when that happens, requiring a clear trace and dry landing at the named
height. It still checks projected run-off.

The previous shelf candidate had two deaths after dry descents. Romero
turned south in the air in both tapes after leaving the lower deck's east lip. The ground
guard had a dry straight-line landing, but the combat wish changed the arc.
The follow-up keeps the horizontal velocity for that walk-off until landing.
Ordinary geometry collisions and the separate jump flight paths still apply.

The private QuakeSpasm fixture starts on the deck at (932 -351 -368) with
velocity (258 194 0). It requests yaw 37 on the approach and yaw 230 after
the bot falls below z -370. The unmodified air steering hit lava at about
(1127 -441 -749). The new build maintained the launch velocity until it hit
the east wall and landed dry. All four fixture approaches stayed dry with
the change. The fixture uses real map hulls and a prescribed wish, so the
played ladder tests live combat and routing separately.

QuakeSpasm completed the three middle shelf routes in 8.4, 4.9 and 10.1
seconds and both upper-spawn tasks in 7.5 seconds, all at full health.
All seven settled spawns pass coverage. The lowest directed reach is
119 of 142 seats, or 107 without gated links.
